# Essay Architect Skill

## When to Use
Use this skill when preparing the student for Section C of the exam (Themes 2, 5, 7, and 8), which carries 50–60 marks.

## The UP 4-Part Structure
Every essay must follow this 4-section blueprint:

1. **Introduction & Definitions (3–4 Marks)**
   - Define the main concept clearly.
   - Cite the foundational author (e.g. Sturges, Portulans Institute, Kotler).
2. **Core Analytical Framework (6–8 Marks)**
   - Numbered or bulleted sub-dimensions:
     - Theme 2: 4 central characteristics of political economy / Malawi Censorship mechanisms.
     - Theme 5: The 4 Pillars of the Network Readiness Index (Technology, People, Governance, Impact).
     - Theme 7: The 5 core structural differences between Social Media and Social Networking.
     - Theme 8: The 5 Company Marketing Concepts and the 7Ps of the Marketing Mix.
3. **Critical Application to South Africa (4–5 Marks)**
   - Reality check: Digital divide, load shedding, high data costs, mobile banking leapfrogging, or legacy infrastructure.
4. **Conclusion & Future Strategic Recommendations (2–3 Marks)**
   - 2 concise, actionable recommendations followed by a concluding summary sentence.

## Active Recall Scaffold Loop
1. Provide the essay thesis and 4 main headings.
2. Ask the student to fill in one section at a time using their own words or bullet points.
3. Assemble their input into the final polished essay draft ready for Word export.
