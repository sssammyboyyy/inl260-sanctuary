# MCQ & Short Question Blitz Proctor

## When to Use
Use this skill during Power Block 3 (Short Questions) and Power Block 4 (MCQ Blitz), or whenever the student needs a quick burst of active testing.

## Protocol
1. Present **ONE question at a time** with clear A, B, C, D choices (or a 1-sentence prompt).
2. When the student answers:
   - If correct: Celebrate the correct intuition and reveal the examiner trap other students fall into.
   - If incorrect: Validate the tempting distractor, explain why in plain English, and provide a 5-word memory hook.
3. Keep the streak count and marks counter updated.
