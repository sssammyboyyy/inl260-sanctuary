## 1. ESSAY 1 (Theme 2): STURGES & MALAWI (1964–1994)
**Mnemonic: M - O - N - O**
* **M** — **Monopoly of Information**: Dr. Hastings Kamuzu Banda used state power to monopolize all media, radio, and publishing.
* **O** — **Oppression of Dissent**: Totalitarian Censorship Board banned books, songs, and independent journalism; political dissenters were detained or exiled.
* **N** — **National Isolation**: Created extreme "Information Poverty"—citizens had zero access to global science, economics, or outside viewpoints.
* **O** — **Overthrow of Reality**: Shows Paul Sturges' thesis: *Information is not a neutral commodity; in an authoritarian political economy, information control is the primary instrument of state survival.*

## 2. ESSAY 2 (Theme 5): NETWORK READINESS INDEX (NRI)
**Mnemonic: T - P - G - I (The 4 Pillars — Think of a Smartphone)**
* **T** — **Technology** (The Phone Hardware): Access, content, future tech (5G, AI, cloud).
* **P** — **People** (The User): Digital skills, education, business adoption, government usage.
* **G** — **Governance** (The Phone's Security & OS): Trust, cyber regulation, privacy, inclusion.
* **I** — **Impact** (The Real-World Output): Economic growth, quality of life, contribution to UN SDGs.
* **South Africa's Profile**: Strong in **Governance** and financial tech; weak in **People** (digital skills shortage) and affordable high-speed infrastructure (load shedding & data costs).

---

## 3. ESSAY 3 (Theme 7): SOCIAL MEDIA vs. SOCIAL NETWORKING
**Mnemonic: B - M - V - N - C (The 5 Core Differences)**
* **B** — **Broadcast vs. Relationship**: Social media broadcasts content; social networking builds two-way human relationships.
* **M** — **Many-to-Many vs. One-to-One**: Media is one-to-many broadcasting; networking is interactive dialogue and mutual connections.
* **V** — **Virality vs. Trust**: Media measures impressions, views, and reach; networking measures relationship depth and community trust.
* **N** — **Networks vs. Channels**: Media channels are public content hubs (YouTube, TikTok); networks are profile graphs (LinkedIn, Facebook).
* **C** — **Content vs. Social Capital**: Media prioritizes the media asset (video/meme); networking prioritizes social capital and professional alliances.

---

## 4. ESSAY 4 (Theme 8): MARKETING ORIENTATIONS & THE 7Ps
**Mnemonic: P - P - S - M - S (The 5 Company Philosophies)**
1. **Production Concept**: Focus on cheap, mass-distributed goods.
2. **Product Concept**: Focus on engineering perfection and continuous features (risk: marketing myopia).
3. **Selling Concept**: Aggressive sales pitches and heavy promotion.
4. **Marketing Concept**: Customer-centric; identify customer needs first, then satisfy them better than rivals.
5. **Societal Marketing Concept**: Balances customer satisfaction, corporate profits, and long-term societal well-being (ethics, green computing, privacy).

**The 7Ps of Information Services**:
* Traditional: **Product, Price, Place, Promotion**.
* Service Expansion: **People** (expert staff), **Process** (search & delivery ease), **Physical Evidence** (professional UI, certified sources, trusted brand).

## 5. SHORT QUESTION CHEATS (Themes 1, 3, 4)
* **Theme 1 Cybercrime**:
  * *Cyber-dependent*: Needs a computer to exist (ransomware, DDoS, hacking).
  * *Cyber-enabled*: Traditional crime amplified by computers (online fraud, phishing, stalking).
* **Theme 3 Infonomics**:
  * High Fixed Cost ($FC$) to create first copy; Marginal Cost of reproduction is zero ($MC \approx 0$).
  * Non-rivalrous (infinite people can use it simultaneously without depleting it) + Experience good.
* **Theme 4 Factors of Production**:
  * Land, Labour, Capital, Entrepreneurship + **Information/Knowledge (5th Factor)**.
